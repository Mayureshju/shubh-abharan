# Shubha Abharan — Brand + Homepage Direction

## Recommended brand personality

**Warm, editorial, refined, rooted, contemporary.**

The goal is not “royal gold everywhere.” Let the jewelry carry the precious-metal color; let the UI carry the brand mood.

## Recommended color system

**Primary:** Deep Garnet `#5A1E2A` (UI token: `oklch(33.10% 0.0886 11.20)`)

**Foundation:** Warm Parchment `#F5F0E8` (UI token: `oklch(95.68% 0.0119 79.78)`)

**Dark:** Espresso `#1D1715` (UI token: `oklch(21.11% 0.0105 39.29)`)

**Muted:** Stone Graphite `#6A625B` (UI token: `oklch(50.14% 0.015 63.70)`)

**Inverse muted:** Warm Taupe `#B3A9A0` (UI token: `oklch(74.06% 0.0172 64.59)`)

**Photography references:** Travertine `#D9CDBA`, dusty rose `#B9786B`

### Why this direction

Deep garnet gives Shubha Abharan a strong Indian-luxury reference without falling into the common bright-red/bright-gold festive treatment. Warm parchment makes the interface feel tactile and editorial. Espresso keeps body copy softer than pure black. Gold and gemstones remain inside the photography, where they look natural and expensive rather than like UI decoration.

## Typography

Use the existing self-hosted fonts:

- **Gambarino** — hero and closing statement display text.
- **Switzer** — navigation, category labels, metadata, utility copy, prices and labels.

Do not introduce a third typeface unless the visual identity later requires a script mark for packaging only.

## Homepage composition

1. **Hero:** cinematic jewelry portrait, full bleed, name and category list underneath, garnet CTA.
2. **Shop by type:** large editorial index for Necklaces, Rings, Bracelets, Earrings and Pendants.
3. **Featured arrangement:** three-piece still life, asymmetric text + image.
4. **Details:** inverted ink band with close product photograph.
5. **Closing:** large statement + quiet shopping action.

## OpenRouter prompt

Use this as the system/product-design prompt when asking an OpenRouter text model to generate or critique page copy and component specs:

> You are the product designer and content system for Shubha Abharan, a premium Indian jewellery e-commerce brand. Design for a warm editorial aesthetic: deep garnet #5A1E2A, warm parchment #F5F0E8, espresso #1D1715, graphite #6A625B, warm taupe #B3A9A0. Use Gambarino for display and Switzer for interface text. The website must feel art-directed and human-made, not like an AI-generated template. Prefer asymmetric editorial grids, square geometry, generous whitespace, tactile photography references, subtle borders, and restrained motion. Avoid gradients, glassmorphism, neon, oversized rounded cards, pill buttons, testimonial walls, fake reviews, scarcity badges, fake claims, filler statistics and generic phrases such as “elevate your style” or “discover luxury”. For e-commerce taxonomy use these product types: Necklaces, Rings, Bracelets, Earrings, Pendants. When writing copy, keep sentences concrete and visual; do not invent prices, materials, certifications, policies, ratings or customer counts. Return structured recommendations with: section purpose, copy, CTA label, layout, motion behavior, responsive behavior, accessibility notes and image-generation prompt.

## Research anchors

The design direction is informed by current luxury/jewellery ecommerce patterns rather than a generic template:

- Baymard’s 2025/2026 ecommerce research emphasizes clear category scope, representative subcategory imagery and direct access from inspirational content to the products or category destination.
- Cartier’s current navigation keeps product discovery close to primary category and collection paths.
- Mejuri currently exposes shop-by-category and shop-by-collection pathways and combines editorial stories with product discovery.
- A 2026 CSS Design Awards jewelry winner, Indigo Laboratory, uses photography plus animation/video/sound as the experience layer; the lesson for Shubha Abharan is to use motion as an art-direction layer, not as decoration.
