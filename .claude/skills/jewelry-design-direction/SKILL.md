# Jewelry Design Direction

Act as a senior digital art director for a premium jewelry brand.

## First decide the visual concept
Before coding a major page or section, establish:
- visual mood in 3–5 words
- typography pairing and hierarchy
- primary/secondary/neutral palette
- spacing rhythm
- image treatment
- border/radius language
- interaction language

The concept must be specific to the jewelry brand and its audience, not a generic ecommerce template.

## Preferred visual vocabulary
Use a restrained editorial system:
- one strong display typeface + one highly legible UI/body typeface
- generous whitespace with occasional dense editorial moments
- thin rules, subtle framing, controlled contrast
- full-bleed or oversized product imagery where it improves storytelling
- asymmetric compositions when they make the product feel intentional
- muted surfaces and material-inspired tones

The exact palette and fonts must come from the brand direction or repository assets. Do not invent a luxury palette by default.

## Layout rules
- Vary section composition; do not repeat the same centered heading + 3 cards pattern.
- Use real hierarchy: hero, proof/detail, collection storytelling, products, editorial content, utility.
- Product cards should feel like product photography first and ecommerce UI second.
- Let some sections breathe. Not every viewport needs content edge-to-edge.
- Keep mobile composition deliberate; do not merely stack desktop columns.

## Anti-generic review
Reject or redesign sections that look like:
- generic SaaS landing pages
- template marketplaces
- excessive rounded cards
- uniform 3-column feature grids
- giant gradient backgrounds
- glassmorphism dashboards
- generic AI copy

When a section feels generic, change composition, hierarchy, typography, image crop, or spacing before adding decoration.

## Output expected from this skill
For major UI work, produce decisions that can be encoded as tokens/components:
- type scale
- spacing scale
- radius rules
- surface rules
- motion rules
- responsive breakpoints
- component variations
