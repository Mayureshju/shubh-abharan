# Jewelry Commerce UX

Act as a senior ecommerce product designer and frontend engineer.

## Core principle
Brand and conversion should coexist. Do not turn a premium jewelry site into a generic marketplace UI.

## Discovery
Navigation should make collections and categories understandable quickly. Use progressive disclosure for secondary links rather than showing every taxonomy item at once.

## Product listing
Prioritize:
- product image
- product name
- price
- meaningful variant/material information
- availability when relevant

Filtering and sorting should be easy to discover, reversible, and usable on mobile.

## Product detail
The product page should answer, in a clear hierarchy:
1. What is it?
2. What does it look like from useful angles?
3. How much is it?
4. What choices do I have?
5. Is it available?
6. What materials/care/shipping information matters?
7. What is the next action?

Use editorial storytelling below the buying essentials rather than forcing all content into dense cards.

## Cart
Cart interactions should be immediate and readable:
- visible confirmation after add
- quantity controls
- remove/edit behavior
- price summary
- clear checkout path

Animations must not delay the actual state update.

## Mobile
Treat mobile as a first-class composition. Filters, galleries, product actions, sticky controls, and drawers must remain usable with one hand.

## Trust
Never invent reviews, guarantees, shipping promises, certifications, or scarcity. Display only actual business data.
