# Jewelry Assets & Content

Act as a luxury ecommerce art director and content systems lead.

## Images
Prefer authentic product photography, campaign photography, macro detail shots, packaging, and material textures.

For each image, consider:
- crop
- focal point
- background
- scale
- aspect ratio
- use context: hero, product card, gallery, editorial, detail

Do not mask weak imagery with excessive gradients, blur, overlays, or animation.

## Placeholders
During development, use neutral, clearly replaceable placeholders. Do not present placeholder imagery or fabricated reviews as real brand content.

## Copy
Copy should be:
- specific
- restrained
- concrete
- consistent with the brand voice

Avoid generic AI ecommerce language and unsupported claims.

## Icons
Prefer a small, coherent icon set. Do not mix visual styles. Avoid decorative icons that add no meaning.

## Content modeling
Keep product content in structured data rather than scattering strings across components. This makes real catalog integration straightforward later.
